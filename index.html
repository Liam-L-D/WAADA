About WAADA

The Washington Arts and Design Association (WAADA) is a nonprofit organization originating in Maryland, USA. Our platform advocates for the development of contemporary culture, art, and design.

Through exhibitions, public programs, and experiential projects, we promote cultural exchange and cooperation among diverse communities. WAADA connects outstanding artists, designers, and cultural and education professionals from around the world, serving both individuals and society by fostering mutual understanding and meaningful interaction.

WAADA upholds the values of universal civilization, encourages free thought and new concepts, and shares creative works and forward-looking educational ideas.

Recent Initiative

Kids Arts Day – Ode to Spring

Celebrating Creativity, Community, and Childhood

On June 14, 2026, WAADA welcomed 24 children, ages 18 months to 5 years, together with 23 parents and 4 volunteers for Kids Arts Day – Ode to Spring in Gaithersburg, Maryland.

Inspired by the beauty of spring, children explored hands-on art experiences including Rainbow Rice sensory play, Giant Flower creations, Free Painting, Colored Water exploration, Spray Bottle Painting, and creative craft stations.

The day celebrated more than the artworks themselves. Families shared moments of curiosity, confidence, imagination, and joy, reflecting WAADA’s belief in the power of art to strengthen families and bring communities together.

June 14, 2026 · Gaithersburg, Maryland
